Health Tips

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily. More if you're training intensely. Proper hydration improves performance and recovery.

Prioritize Sleep

Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep. Growth hormone is released during deep sleep, which is crucial for muscle recovery.

Protein Timing

Consume 20-30g of protein within 30 minutes after your workout to maximize muscle protein synthesis and recovery.

Active Recovery

On rest days, do light activities like walking or stretching. This promotes blood flow and speeds up recovery.

Mind-Muscle Connection

Focus on the muscle you're working during each exercise. This mental focus can significantly improve muscle activation.

Progressive Overload

Gradually increase weight, reps, or sets over time. This is the key principle for continuous strength and muscle gains.
