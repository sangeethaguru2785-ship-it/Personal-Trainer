Nutrition Guide

Understand Your Daily Calories

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) determines how many calories your body needs each day. Knowing this helps you lose weight, maintain, or build muscle effectively.

Caloric Deficit Eat less than you burn to lose weight
Maintenance Eat equal to what you burn to stay steady
Caloric Surplus Eat more than you burn to build muscle
Activity Level Higher activity = more daily calories needed
