My Story

From Athlete to Your Trainer

Growing up, sport was my everything. I played college football, competed in bodybuilding, and discovered that my true passion wasn't just training myself — it was helping others unlock potential they didn't know they had.

After earning my certifications and spending years learning from the best coaches in the industry, I founded Stackly with one mission: to give every person access to the same world-class coaching that elite athletes receive. Today, I've helped over 500 clients transform their bodies, their confidence, and their lives.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

My Mission

To empower every individual I work with to discover their strongest, healthiest self — not through quick fixes, but through sustainable habits, science-backed programming, and a coach who genuinely believes in them. I don't just train bodies; I build confidence that carries into every area of life.

My Vision

To create a world where fitness is celebrated, not feared. Where every person, regardless of age, background, or ability, has access to the knowledge and support they need to transform their health. I envision Stackly as the gold standard for personal training — where results meet real human connection.

Philosophy

My Fitness Philosophy

The core beliefs that drive every program I design and every session I coach.

Mind Over Muscle

Physical transformation starts mentally. I focus on building the mindset, discipline, and self-belief that make results not just possible — inevitable.

Progress, Not Perfection

Every rep, every healthy meal, every extra hour of sleep is a win. I program for consistent progress — not crash diets or unsustainable extremes.

Train Smart, Not Just Hard

Effort matters, but so does strategy. I use data, recovery science, and periodization to make sure your hard work actually pays off without burnout or injury.

Fuel the Machine

Nutrition isn't an afterthought — it's half the battle. I teach clients to eat with purpose: enough protein, enough calories, and zero guilt.

Recovery Is Training

Your body grows when you rest, not when you train. Sleep, mobility, and stress management are built into every program I create.

Community Fuels Growth

The right environment accelerates results. I build coaching relationships based on trust, accountability, and the shared belief that we're better together.

Experience

My Journey

Key moments that shaped who I am as a trainer and a person.

2006

College Football & First Passion

Played linebacker at university and discovered my love for strength training. Started helping teammates with their programs and realized coaching was my calling.

2009

First Certification & Gym Floor

Earned my NASM-CPT and started training clients on a commercial gym floor. Learned fast that real coaching is about listening, adapting, and building trust.

2013

Bodybuilding Competition Era

Competed in multiple natural bodybuilding shows and placed top 3 in my class. This gave me deep firsthand knowledge of contest prep, extreme fat loss, and mental toughness.

2016

Stackly Founded

Opened the first Stackly studio with a vision: bring elite-level coaching to everyday people. Started with 12 clients and a dream.

2020

Went Fully Online

Built a remote coaching platform during the pandemic. Took on clients from 12+ countries and developed a proven online transformation system.

2024

500+ Lives Changed

Reached the milestone of 500+ documented client transformations. Recognized by industry peers as one of the top independent trainers in New York.
